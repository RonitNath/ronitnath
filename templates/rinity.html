Rinity

The line stays open

The phone gets answered, even when your front desk cannot.

Rinity is a receptionist for dental practices that takes the calls your staff miss — booking appointments, taking messages, and answering the questions you have already answered a hundred times. What it could not handle is waiting for you, with the recording.

Rinity is being prepared for its next release. Existing practices will receive direct transition details.

01

Answers every call

Rinity picks up when the front desk is with a patient, after hours, and through the lunch hour. Callers hear a receptionist, not a menu.

02

Books into your schedule

It offers the times your providers actually have open, holds the slot while the caller decides, and writes the appointment to the day it belongs on.

03

Flags what needs a person

Anything it could not finish — a message, an unanswered question, or a booking that went sideways — waits for review with the recording and transcript.

Rinity is built by Isoastra.

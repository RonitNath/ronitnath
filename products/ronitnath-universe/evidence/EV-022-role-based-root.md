"At base, I don't want a /app style routing. The content you see is based on what user you are. So, everyone lands at ronitnath.com as their root, and what they see depends on what their role is."

One canonical URL. No `/app`, no `/admin`, no redirect dance. `ronitnath.com` resolves per role:

| Role | Root renders |
| --- | --- |
| VISITOR | presence — the night-sky language, unchanged |
| OWNER | the **personal dashboard** (new — see F-12) |
| FRIEND (later bet) | their own card set on the same surface |

Also settled in the same breath: **the "root admin page" is a personal dashboard, and it is a different thing from the per-event dashboard** (F-6). It is a **routing and lightweight health surface** — not a control panel. Long-term it is the hub root for the private social platform the site is meant to become (EV-009), so its structure must leave room for photos, calendar and circles to arrive as additional cards without a redesign.

Clarification recorded because it resolves a misunderstanding: when the owner said "base page" at the design gate he meant **the first Penpot page**, not the application's root route. The base-screen objections raised by DEBATE-002 C-10 were partly answering a question he hadn't asked.

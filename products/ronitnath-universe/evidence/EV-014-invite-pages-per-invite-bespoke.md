Invite pages are bespoke **per invite**, not merely per event: the owner reorders and organizes modules "depending on what's more crucial depending on the event **and the people I'm inviting**," and may build custom components for specific functionality. Consequence for the model: page composition varies by *audience*, so a page doc cannot be a single per-event singleton — ordering/selection must be expressible per invite (or per invite-group), and the module registry must accept event-specific custom components as a normal path (extends DEBATE-001 C-6 from per-event to per-invite).

Consequence for the design gate: the invite page is NOT the base screen — it has no single canonical layout to lock. The **admin overview** is the base screen (owner ruling), and it is the surface that carries the platform's design language.

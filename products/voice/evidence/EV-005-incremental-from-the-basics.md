"I want to incrementally build this, starting from the basics, so I can ensure the quality of the pipeline and components."

Sets the build method and names its purpose: bottom-up, smallest pieces first, so the owner
can personally verify quality at each layer. The stated reason is inspection, not risk
management — the increments exist so there is something for him to judge. Consequence: the
bet's bound is denominated in owner review sessions (SYS-DEC-001) with a natural cadence of
one session per component layer, and each work packet must land something the owner can
exercise and form an opinion about, not an internal milestone. Also constrains scope: a
big-bang port of audgent's feature surface is ruled out by construction. Open: what "the
basics" starts at — audio transport, the frame core, or a single end-to-end call.

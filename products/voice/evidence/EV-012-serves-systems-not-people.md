"This is going to serve products like r i n i t y, and, essentially, only systems will plug into it."

touches this product directly, and no customer-facing surface belongs to it. Consequence:
the external contract is an API/integration seam designed for programs, and every question
about UX for callers is out of scope — the consuming product owns that. This narrows
audgent/EV-006 ("agents are the primary interfacing surface") rather than repeating it:
there, agents authored and operated the engine; here, the plug-in surface is for products,
while the owner's own inspection surface is separate (EV-013).

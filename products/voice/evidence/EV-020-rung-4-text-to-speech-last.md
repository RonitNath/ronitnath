"And then finally, only after all of that is the text to speech system."

Places TTS last on the ladder, after ingest/replay, segmentation/STT, and the LLM turn.
The word "only" is doing work: no speech output exists until everything beneath it is
hardened. Consequence: for most of this bet the system cannot talk, and no packet may pull
TTS forward to make a demo feel complete — that instinct is precisely the console failure
(EV-009). Completing this rung is what first makes the system a voice agent; the rungs
above it (EV-021 .. EV-026) make it an intelligent one.

"Once I'm happy with the internals, I'll graduate it to Isoastra."

Names the exit condition and who judges it: the owner, on the quality of the internals —
not on feature parity with audgent, not on a customer landing, not on uptime. Graduation to
Isoastra is a later, separate event. Consequence: the success signal for this bet is an
owner verdict on the pipeline's construction (EV-005), which makes "what would make you
happy with the internals" a mandatory interview question — an unstated quality bar cannot be
built against. It also means the product must be designed to move: hostname, identity realm,
and branding are configuration, not assumptions baked through the code, so graduation is a
promotion rather than a second rewrite.

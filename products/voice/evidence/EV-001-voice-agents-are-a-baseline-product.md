"Let's go PM mode on voice agents as a baseline product."

Establishes voice agents as a product in their own right rather than a capability inside
rinity or frontdesk-dental. "Baseline" reads as foundational — the layer other products
consume, whose quality is judged on its own terms before any consumer is attached.
Consequence: this gets its own portfolio entry, its own evidence log, and its own bet;
consumers (rinity, frontdesk-dental, the owner's projects) are downstream and do not get
to define its shape by their delivery deadlines. Open for the interview: whether this
product supersedes the audgent entry outright or coexists with it during a transition.

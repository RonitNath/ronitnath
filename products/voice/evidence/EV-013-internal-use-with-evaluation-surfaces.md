Defines what the web surface is for and what standard it is held to. Its job is evaluation:
enough metrics and views that the owner can form a judgment about the product. Its licence
is that aesthetics are explicitly not a requirement — ugly and developer-friendly is
sanctioned, because the audience is one person. Consequence: the design gate for this bet
is about information sufficiency and honesty (EV-009), not visual craft; the usual interface
standards (`feedback_interface_minimalism` and friends) apply only where they serve
legibility. Note the tension to hold: not-for-others licenses ugliness, it does not license
theater — the surface must still be trustworthy.

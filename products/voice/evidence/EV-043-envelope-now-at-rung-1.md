"Envelope-now"

Resolves the one escalation the debate could not settle, between EV-041 (schema churn destroys
measurement) and EV-009 (machinery built ahead of its producers is what killed console). Rung 1
creates `audio_clips`, `runs`, `signal_definitions` and `observations`, and its only signals are
waveform, duration, sample rate and channels — real producers, so the envelope is exercised
rather than speculative. Explicitly held back until something produces them: registry
compatibility edges, coverage/gap observations, and comparison contracts. Consequence: the
observation envelope, the two time coordinates, run manifests and subject attribution are fixed
at rung 1 and everything later is additive; and DEBATE-001's C-15 is settled with it — a
waveform is an observation, not a storage fact. The knowingly accepted cost is four table names
that code will depend on before rung 2 proves them right.

"Right now, it's configured for what's needed for it to be a self-hostable vapi or retell alternative. However, this isn't really what I need from it."

Rejects the inherited product identity. audgent's shape today is dograh's shape: a
self-hostable clone of a commercial voice-agent platform, built to be operated by strangers
for workloads its authors never see. That framing — not any single feature — is what the
owner is discarding. Consequence: every surface that exists because a generic hosted product
needed it (self-serve config breadth, provider-agnostic parity, tenant isolation, the console
as a product) is now up for re-justification against internal need rather than defended as
table stakes. Inventory of what "as configured" means is EV-011 in the rinity product.

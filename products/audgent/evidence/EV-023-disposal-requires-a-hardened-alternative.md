"This project will get disposed of when I have a hardened and production tested alternative. Don't assume anytime soon."

Sets the retirement condition and, more importantly, the horizon. Disposal is gated on
`voice` being **hardened and production-tested** — not merely built, not merely working —
and the owner explicitly warns against treating that as near. Qualifies EV-007: audgent is
interim in *role* but not in *duration*, so "it's a stopgap" is not an argument for cheap,
throwaway work. The correct posture is work that stays maintainable and legible for an
indefinite stretch, while staying inside the discussed scope (EV-021). Consequence for the
appetite argument: cost of ownership over a long horizon outweighs cost of construction, which
inverts the tradeoff a short-stopgap reading would suggest.

"what this current voice platform is is just a good simple way for us to have working voice agents for whatever external products that we want to sell to other people such as [rinity]."

States the job plainly: audgent supplies working voice agents that sellable products stand on.
Not a platform in its own right, not a thing anyone buys — plumbing under products that are
bought. "Good simple way" is a quality bar as much as a description: the measure is whether a
voice agent works and can be gotten working quickly, not whether the engine is capable in the
abstract. Consequence: features that don't shorten the distance between "a product needs a
voice agent" and "the voice agent is running correctly" are off-thesis for this bet.

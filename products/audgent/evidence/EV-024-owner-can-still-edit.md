"I should still be able to make edits, even though I do it very infrequently."

Qualifies EV-012. Configuration being agent work does not make the human surface
inspection-only: the owner keeps direct edit capability, and exercises it rarely. The
distinction is **frequency, not permission** — agents are the normal path (EV-006, EV-011),
direct editing is the exception that must nonetheless exist. Consequence: the five reads
(EV-014) are the human surface's center of gravity but not its whole extent; editing
capability survives wherever configuration is displayed, and the design problem becomes
keeping an infrequent path available without letting it shape the layout. Also resolves
FLOWS-001/F-6: the flow stays, and gains a direct-edit branch rather than being the only
way to close the loop.

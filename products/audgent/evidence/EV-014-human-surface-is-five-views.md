"in a human surface, I'm mainly looking at what are the calls going through, what are the call logs, what are the costs, what are the configurations, and what have the agents been up to in terms of making configurations."

Enumerates the human surface as five reads: **live calls in flight, call logs, costs,
current configuration, and the agents' configuration activity**. All five are inspection;
none is authoring — consistent with EV-012. The fifth is the novel one and has no equivalent
in the inherited console: a history of what the agents changed, which is the owner's only
handle on a system he no longer configures himself. Consequence: this list is the design
gate's screen inventory for the human plane, and each view must be answerable across the
whole system, not one workspace (EV-010). Costs must break down by production vs. agent-test
(EV-013).

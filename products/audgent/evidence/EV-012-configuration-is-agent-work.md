"I just see a lot of these provider configurations and it's built in a way for you to be able to, like, plug your own keys in and then configure, but this is not the workflow I want. I essentially am just having my agents do the configuration in the first place."

Kills BYOK self-serve configuration as the interaction model. The inherited console assumes a
human customer arrives with their own vendor keys and fills in forms; here the keys are the
owner's already and the filling-in is agent work. Consequence: provider configuration screens
lose their reason to exist as *input* surfaces and become *readback* surfaces — the owner
reads what the agents configured (EV-014), rather than configuring it. Sharpens EV-004: the
interface's job is inspection, not data entry.

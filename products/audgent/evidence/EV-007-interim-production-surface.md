"there's another project going on right now to make a proper Iso[astra] internal voice agent platform, but [audgent] needs to be working right now because this is the production surface, and this is what we can use right now while the other product is still being built."

Fixes audgent's position in time: it is the **interim** production surface, held in service
until the greenfield `voice` product replaces it. This is the single most bound-setting fact
of the bet — every proposal is now judged against "does this keep production working until
voice lands", not "does this make audgent good". Consequence: investment that would only pay
off over a long horizon belongs in `voice`, not here; and anything built here should be
cheap to abandon. Qualifies EV-002 — audgent serves internal projects, but as a stopgap, and
`voice` is the durable answer to the same need.

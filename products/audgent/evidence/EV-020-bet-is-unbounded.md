"No dates." / "No bounds."

The owner declines to bound this bet — neither a real-world deadline nor a review-session
budget. This is a deliberate waiver of SYS-DEC-001's requirement, not an omission, and it is
the one place this bet departs from the operating system. What the waiver costs, named so it
isn't rediscovered: the bound is the mechanism that makes scope flex instead of grow, and
"budget exhausted → bet stops and is re-pitched" has nothing to fire on. Scope control here
therefore has to come from the no-go list (EV-021) and the fixed capability set already
discussed, since it cannot come from the budget. Consequence for the debate line: T2 (bound
size) can't be evaluated; the pitch records the waiver explicitly and rests its debate
decision on the other triggers.

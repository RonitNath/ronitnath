"per office. We're targetting a higher end business, not going for low margins."

Settles pricing shape and market posture: **per-office flat pricing**, positioned high-end.
Consequences: no per-call/per-minute metering surface is needed for billing (usage metering
remains an ops concern, not a pricing one); the product must *feel* premium — the experience
is part of what justifies the price (connects to EV-012's experience frame and the EV-026
quality bar); an office is the billing unit, reinforcing office as the tenancy atom (EV-010
spine). Low-margin volume plays (cheap answering-service tier) are not the target customer.

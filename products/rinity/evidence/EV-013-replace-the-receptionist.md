"replace the receptionist"

Settles the scope of the bet in three words: rinity's job is to replace the receptionist —
not to assist one, not to catch overflow, not to be an after-hours answering service. The
receptionist's full duty surface is the requirements source: answering, scheduling,
rescheduling/cancelling, insurance questions, new-patient intake, messages to the back
office, recall, confirmations. Anything a practice's receptionist does that rinity can't do
is a gap by definition. This is the strongest possible framing of EV-002's "we will solve
this business operation for you" for the phone-line operation.

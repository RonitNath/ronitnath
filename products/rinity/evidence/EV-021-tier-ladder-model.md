"each step up is clearing one thing the product could be, and a lower tier which can be sold."

Settles the scope model for the unbounded bet (EV-014): comprehensiveness is a **ladder of
sellable tiers**. Each tier, once cleared, is (a) a product that can be sold as-is and (b) the
prerequisite for the next thing the product could become. The pitch must therefore define the
tiers explicitly — what each one is as a sellable product, what it clears the way for — rather
than choosing a single scope cut. There is no tier that exists only as a stepping stone; if it
can't be sold, it isn't a tier boundary.

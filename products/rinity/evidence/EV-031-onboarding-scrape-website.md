"we need a solid onboarding process that we spec beforehand. Further, it ought to accept a
practice's website and then scrape all the necessary information, and only ask the user for
what it couldn't figure out."

Context for the bar: "ai-assistant has previously shown rcda a good product" — RCDA has seen
polished AI product before; onboarding must meet that standard.

Onboarding is a **spec'd-beforehand** surface (design-gate work, not emergent): signup takes
the practice's website URL, scrapes hours, services, providers, insurance, location, tone —
everything configurable — and asks the owner **only for what scraping couldn't determine**.
The gap-filling interview is the fallback, not the default.

Settles the product's market frame: rinity is not a dental product that might expand — it is a
healthcare voice-agent product whose first segment is dental. Positioning, naming, and any
user-facing surface must read healthcare-wide; dental specifics live in configuration/verticals,
not in the product's identity. Also settles that rinity is the flagship: it is the only brand
Isoastra pushes, so its bar is the company's bar.

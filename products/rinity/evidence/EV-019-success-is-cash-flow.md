"cash flow"

not founder satisfaction — money moving. Consequences: the RCDA pilot (EV-005) is a step, not
the win; the bet is only won when a practice pays, which per EV-016 (self-serve) and EV-018

sellable, sign-up-able product carrying real patient traffic. This makes the founder's bar
(EV-004) concrete from the outside: worth deploying and marketing ≈ worth paying for.
